I'm a final-year PhD researcher at Northumbria University, working on explainable artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on counterfactual explanations, uncertainty quantification, and applications of machine learning in environmental sciences. I'm particularly interested in developing machine learning techniques which represent the structure of real world problems, rather than relying on data driven correlations.

You can find my publications [here](/publications/), and code from my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/jacob-ws).
